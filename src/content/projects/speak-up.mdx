## The problem

Students have ideas about their school, but a suggestion box loses them and a meeting only hears the loudest voices. Prefects need a way to collect ideas, see which ones matter to most students, and show that something happened.

Speak Up is Dubai College's student parliament, online.

## Built for a school

Five roles, from student to a master account, each see and do only what they should. Anonymous posts stay anonymous to everyone, including the people running it. Sign-in limits are tuned for a whole school sharing one network: five attempts a minute per student, fifty per address.<Note>Set in <code>constants.py</code>, with the policy tested in the app's test suite.</Note> A strict content security policy allows no inline scripts, and the app refuses to leave itself without an administrator.
