## The problem

Students applying to study law in the UK are told to read widely, think critically and show it. In practice that means a dozen news sites, a reading list, a notebook, and no way to see how anyone else thought about the same story.

JurisLearning puts that in one place, and turns reading into a portfolio.

Alongside the feed there is a library of more than 40 landmark cases explained in plain English, a reviewed reading list, and full preparation for the LNAT admissions test, including a bank of 40 essay titles with planning angles.

## Engineering

The backend is asynchronous FastAPI on PostgreSQL. News and journal feeds are ingested on a schedule,<Note>News every 2 hours, journals every 6, in <code>backend/app/tasks/scheduler.py</code>.</Note> with source health checks, sanitised HTML, rate limiting and an admin dashboard for moderation. Database migrations run before every deploy, and CI fails if the models and migrations drift apart. The whole stack deploys from a single blueprint: database, API, web app and storage.

JurisLearning is about to launch.
