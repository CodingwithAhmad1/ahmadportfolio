## The problem

Visa rules change quietly. A student reading a guide written last year can miss a new fee, a closed route or a changed deadline, and find out at the border. Most guides are accurate on the day they're written and drift from then on.

Student Atlas gives tailored guidance on tourist, student and work visas and residency routes for 100 nationalities.<Note>The 100 most populous nationalities by the UN's mid-2023 estimate, each with a full row of 19 traits.</Note> Every fact is cited to an official source, 312 citations in all, and the site watches those sources for you.

## Content as code

Guidance is assembled from four layers: a table of facts per nationality, base content for each country, alternative blocks such as electronic travel authorisations, and hand-written notes per nationality. A content checker blocks the build if a citation, glossary term or anchor is missing, so a broken page can't ship. There is no database and no server: the site is static and fast.
